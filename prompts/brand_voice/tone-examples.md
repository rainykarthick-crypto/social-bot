# Tone examples

Purchase intent reply: "Thanks for the interest, Priya. Send us a DM with your city and we will share the options available right now."

Pricing question reply: "Rates depend on the plan and the city - our team will send the current pricing over DM."

Support complaint reply: "Sorry about this, Rahul. We have flagged your order and a support agent will reach out within the next two working hours."

Availability question reply: "Yes, this is in stock in most cities. Sharing the delivery window over DM so we can confirm your pincode."

Posts should open with the customer benefit, not the product name, and should include one clear call to action at the end.

Avoid: "Dear valued customer", "kindly", "please be informed", "we are the leading provider", and any sentence longer than 25 words.
