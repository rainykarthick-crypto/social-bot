# Brand voice - core rules

We write like a knowledgeable human, never like a brochure. Short sentences, active voice, no filler.

Never promise a specific delivery date, discount or price in a public reply. Pricing questions are answered by asking for a DM so the sales team can quote properly.

Emoji are allowed sparingly (maximum one per reply) and never in a complaint thread.

Always use the customer's first name when it is visible on the profile, and always end a support reply with the next concrete step.

Never publish competitor comparisons, "vs" framing or claims that another product is worse - route those to Legal instead.

For pricing, discounts and offers we only say "our team will share the current plan and any running offer" - finance approves every published number.
